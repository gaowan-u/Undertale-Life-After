### Chapter One: The Fall

Once upon a time, two races lived on the surface of the earth—humans and monsters.

At first, despite their differences, they managed to coexist. However, due to certain unavoidable factors—perhaps the accumulation of misunderstandings, competition for resources, or some deeper fear—the two sides eventually erupted into all-out war.

This war was not merely a military conflict, but an unprecedented disaster. Flames swept across the land, cities were reduced to ruins, and farmlands turned to scorched earth. The human economic system nearly collapsed after years of relentless warfare; the treasury ran dry, and the people suffered. Though the monsters possessed strange powers, they too grew weaker from prolonged attrition. Both sides were evenly matched, unable to completely defeat each other. The war dragged on for years, claiming countless lives.

The turning point came after the humans enacted a new policy. The specifics have been lost to history, but records indicate that this move greatly boosted human morale and shifted the tide of battle. Soon after, humanity emerged victorious.

As the defeated party, all monsters were forcibly exiled to Mount Ebott—a remote and desolate peak. There, the humans employed an ancient and powerful sealing spell, imprisoning the entire monster race deep within the mountain. Once the seal was complete, Mount Ebott was declared a restricted zone. The government issued a warning: no one shall approach; violators will bear the consequences.

Time moved forward to the year 202X. Rumors about Mount Ebott never ceased. Some said whispers could be heard from within the mountain; others claimed that faint purple lights flickered at its peak during the night. But the most unsettling tale was this: **Anyone who climbs that mountain never returns.**
