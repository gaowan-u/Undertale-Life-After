### Chapter One: The Fall

Once upon a time, two races lived on the surface of the Earth—humans and monsters.

At first, there were barriers between them, but they could still coexist. However, due to certain uncontrollable factors—perhaps the accumulation of misunderstandings, competition over resources, or some deeper fear—the two sides eventually erupted into a full-scale war.

This war was not just a military conflict, but an unprecedented disaster. Flames of war swept across the land, cities were reduced to ruins, and farmlands turned to scorched earth. The human economic system nearly collapsed after years of war; the treasury was depleted, and people’s livelihoods deteriorated. Though the monsters possessed strange powers, they too gradually weakened due to prolonged depletion. Both sides were evenly matched, unable to completely defeat each other, and the war sank into a quagmire, lasting for years with countless casualties.

The turning point came after humans enacted a new policy. The exact details are unknown, but historical records state that this move greatly boosted human morale and reversed the course of the war. Soon after, humans achieved ultimate victory.

As the defeated party, all monsters were forcibly exiled to Mount lpote—a remote and desolate peak. There, humans employed an ancient and powerful sealing technique to imprison the entire monster race deep within the mountain permanently. After the seal was completed, Mount lpote was declared a restricted area, with a government announcement stating: No one may approach, and violators will face the consequences.

Time moved forward to the year 202X. Rumors about Mount Ebott never ceased. Some said that whispers could often be heard from within the mountain; some said that faint purple lights flickered at its peak at night. But the most unsettling rumor was: **Anyone who climbs that mountain never returns.**
